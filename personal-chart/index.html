Personal Chart Maker

Enter the songs you played each week and get a shareable, Hot 100-style chart of your own, with last week, peak, weeks on chart and movement worked out for you.

1. Weeks

Columns needed: Week, Title, Artist, Streams. Week is the date the week ends. Artist is optional.
2. Enter streams for the week
Paste a list instead
Commas or tabs both work, so you can paste straight from a spreadsheet. Artist can be left out.

Songs are ranked by their streams that week, and a song that's already in the week gets its streams updated. A blank or zero count means the song didn't play. Last week, peak and weeks on chart come from the other weeks you've entered.
