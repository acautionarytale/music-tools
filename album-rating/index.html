    Save and load

    Save everything on the page (details, songs, ratings, album score, style and cover) as one small file, and load it later to keep editing. Loading replaces what is on the page now. Your work is also saved automatically in this browser, but export is the way to back it up or move it to another device.

    If the download is blocked, press and hold (or right-click) the image to save it.
