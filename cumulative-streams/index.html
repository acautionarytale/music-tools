Cumulative Streams Graph

Plot the running total of streams for several albums, artists or tracks over any stretch of time, and download it as an image.

1. Data

Columns needed: Month (or a date), Name, Streams (Name can be an album, artist or track).
Imports use the data saved in this browser by the Streams table maker, so nothing needs to be typed twice.
Uploads pick their own period from the dates: first-of-month dates are monthly, dates a week apart are weekly, other dates are daily.
2. Enter / edit streams (monthly totals)

Merge duplicates

Spotify counts different editions of the same album separately. Merging adds the first name's numbers into the second and removes it. The pairing is remembered, so later imports merge it automatically.

Enter total streams per month for the year chosen above, then switch years to fill in the rest. Blank counts as 0. Order the rows with ▲▼. You can paste a block of cells from a spreadsheet into any cell and it fills across and down from there.
