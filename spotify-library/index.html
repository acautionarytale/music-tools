Spotify Library

Add your Spotify history once. The Cumulative streams graph, the Streams table maker and the Personal chart maker pull from it, and the merges you make here count everywhere.

1. Your history

Loading…

Choose the zip from Spotify (or the Streaming_History JSON files inside it), or drop them here. Nothing is uploaded; files are read in this browser.

Your history is saved in this browser only. Clearing the site's data would delete it, so back it up now and then. Adding a newer export later only adds plays you don't already have.

2. Settings

Used by every tool when numbers are pulled from your history. Changing it applies the next time you pull.

3. Merge editions and duplicates

Spotify lists each edition as its own album, like “Fearless” and “Fearless (Platinum Edition)”. Pick an artist, tap the tiles that are the same thing, then press Merge. Every tool counts them together afterwards. Your saved plays are never changed, and you can split any merge.

4. Use your history

Cumulative streams graph →Press “Pull from Spotify library” for day, week, month or year, over all time or any stretch. Streams table maker →Press “Import from Spotify” for a table of albums, songs or artists. Personal chart maker →Press “Import from Spotify” to build weekly top 100 charts from any weeks.
