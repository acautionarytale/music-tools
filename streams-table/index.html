Streams Table Maker

Make a shareable PNG of average daily streams by day, week, month or year, with green/red change, for albums, songs or artists. Import from Spotify, upload a streams sheet or type the numbers in.

Uploads pick their own period from the dates: first-of-month dates are monthly, dates a week apart are weekly, other dates are daily.
2. Enter / edit streams (monthly totals)

Merge duplicates

Enter total streams per month. The shaded Dec column is last year's December, used only for January's % change (leave it blank if you don't want that). Blank counts as 0. Order the rows with ▲▼ (that's the order on the chart in "Entry order"). You can paste a block of cells from a spreadsheet into any cell and it fills across and down from there.
