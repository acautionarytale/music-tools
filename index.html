    How it works

    Four small tools, all in your browser

    Music tools
    for your listening notes

    Turn your ratings and stream counts into clean images you can share.

      1. Add your numbersType them in, paste from a spreadsheet, or upload a sheet.
      2. Pick a lookChoose colors, fonts and sizes, or match your album art.
      3. Download the imageSave a PNG to post anywhere, or keep your data to edit later.

      Everything runs in your browser. Each tool saves your work on this device, so use its export or download buttons to keep a copy. The graph tool can import what you entered in the streams table maker.
